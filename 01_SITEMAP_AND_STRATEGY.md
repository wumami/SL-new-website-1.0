## 🔄 User Flow Architecture

### Flow A: Self-Diagnosis (PRIMARY PATH - 60% of conversions)
```
Home Hero "Start Assessment" 
  ↓
Assessment Q&A (3 questions)
  ↓
Recommended Products + Case Studies
  ↓
"Email me this plan" (lead capture)
  ↓
Thank you page with Calendly link
```

### Flow B: Problem-First (25% of conversions)
```
Home "Key Solution Paths" cards
  ↓
/solutions/[specific-problem]
  ↓
Product cards + "Who it's for"
  ↓
Case study inline
  ↓
"Get implementation timeline" (lead form)
```

### Flow C: Product-First (10% - technical buyers)
```
/products/ navigation
  ↓
Product detail page
  ↓
"Technical specs" + Integrations
  ↓
Related case study
  ↓
"Talk to integration team" (lead form)
```

### Flow D: Spencer Risk AI Beta (5% - strategic)
```
Multiple entry points (Home banner, Solutions, Products)
  ↓
/products/spencer-risk-ai
  ↓
Beta program eligibility checklist
  ↓
"Join beta" form (higher qualification)
```

## 🎯 Critical Success Factors

### 1. Assessment Completion Rate (Target: >65%)
- **Why**: Highest-converting path
- **Optimization**: 3 questions max, visual chips (not dropdowns), progress bar

### 2. Lead Form Submission (Target: >35% of Assessment completers)
- **Why**: Core conversion goal
- **Optimization**: 
  - Pre-fill email if signed in
  - "Email me this plan" vs "Submit" (softer language)
  - Checkbox for "20-min call" (not required)

### 3. Spencer Risk AI Beta Applications (Target: 50 qualified leads in 90 days)
- **Why**: Strategic product launch
- **Optimization**:
  - Site-wide banner during beta period
  - Prominent in Assessment results for risk-related bottlenecks
  - Dedicated /products/spencer-risk-ai page with eligibility checklist

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T:
- List all 15 products on homepage
- Use tech jargon ("low-latency aggregation framework")
- Create "Why Spencer Logic" page (nobody cares)
- Add generic "Our Values" content
- Show product screenshots in isolation (no context)

### ✅ DO:
- Start with visitor's problem
- Use outcome language ("Cut manual work by 40%")
- Show products only AFTER diagnosing need
- Pair every product mention with case study link
- Use DriveWealth-style minimal design (white space, trust)

## 📈 A/B Test Roadmap (First 90 Days)

### Week 1-2: Baseline
- Track current conversion funnel
- Heatmaps on Home + Assessment
- Exit intent on Assessment (where do people drop?)

### Week 3-4: Assessment Optimization
**Test A**: 3 questions vs 5 questions
**Test B**: "Start Assessment" vs "Find your solution in 3 clicks"
**Hypothesis**: Shorter = higher completion

### Week 5-6: Lead Form Optimization
**Test A**: Email-only vs Full form (name + company + phone)
**Test B**: "Email me this plan" vs "Get my recommendations"
**Hypothesis**: Less friction = higher submission

### Week 7-8: Spencer Risk AI Positioning
**Test A**: Site-wide banner vs Product-page-only placement
**Test B**: "Join beta" vs "See early results"
**Hypothesis**: Exclusivity increases applications

### Week 9-12: Solution Page Layout
**Test A**: Case study before product cards vs after
**Test B**: Video demo vs Static diagram
**Hypothesis**: Proof before pitch increases trust

## 🎨 Design System Principles (DriveWealth-Inspired)

### Visual Hierarchy:
1. **Primary**: White backgrounds, ample spacing
2. **Accent**: Spencer Logic blue (#4A9FF5 or similar sky blue)
3. **Trust signals**: Subtle, not aggressive (no badges everywhere)

### Typography:
- **Headings**: Clear, confident, NOT salesy
  - Example: "Trading technology that starts with your business problem"
  - NOT: "Revolutionary AI-Powered Next-Gen Trading Solutions"
  
### Imagery:
- **Avoid**: Generic stock photos, complex network diagrams, dark tech aesthetic
- **Use**: Clean UI mockups, simple flow diagrams, real screenshots (if clean)

### Interaction:
- **Hover states**: Subtle (not aggressive)
- **CTA buttons**: Sky blue primary, white/ghost secondary
- **Forms**: Inline validation, friendly error messages

## 🔧 WordPress Implementation Notes

### Required Plugins:
1. **Elementor Pro** (for Assessment wizard + lead forms)
2. **WPForms** (lead capture with hidden field support)
3. **Advanced Custom Fields (ACF)** (for case study templates)
4. **Yoast SEO** (for /resources/blog)
5. **WP Rocket** (performance)

### Custom Templates Needed:
1. `template-assessment.php` (dedicated Assessment page)
2. `template-solution.php` (reusable for all /solutions/* pages)
3. `template-product.php` (reusable for all /products/* pages)
4. `template-case-study.php` (consistent structure)
5. `template-segment.php` (for /who-we-serve/* pages)

### Reusable Components (Elementor):
- **Product Card** (image, title, 2-line description, "Learn more" link)
- **Case Study Card** (title, segment badge, result highlight, "Read story" link)
- **Lead Form Inline** (name, email, company, phone, checkboxes)
- **Solution Path Card** (icon, title, 1-line description, CTA)
- **Recommended Products Result** (appears in Assessment results)

## 📞 Lead Capture Strategy

### Form Types:

#### 1. Assessment Results Form (Highest Priority)
```
Fields:
- First name
- Work email (validated)
- Company
- Phone/WhatsApp (optional)

Hidden fields:
- persona
- goal
- bottleneck
- recommended_products (comma-separated)
- utm_source, utm_campaign (if present)

Checkboxes:
☐ I'd like a 20-minute call to walk through this plan
☐ Send me product updates & insights

CTA: "Email me this plan"
```

#### 2. Spencer Risk AI Beta Form
```
Fields:
- Full name
- Work email
- Company name
- Current role
- Current trading volume (dropdown: <$1B/month, $1-5B, $5B+)
- Do you have a dedicated risk/dealing desk? (Yes/No)
- Biggest risk management challenge (free text, 150 chars)

CTA: "Apply for beta access"

Note: "Limited to 20 partners. Priority given to multi-brand brokers."
```

#### 3. General Demo Request
```
Fields:
- First name
- Work email
- Company
- What are you looking to solve? (dropdown from Solutions)
- Anything else? (optional free text)

CTA: "Request demo"
```

### Post-Submission Behavior:
- **Thank you page**: Show Calendly embed (inline booking)
- **Email autoresponder**: 
  - For Assessment: PDF summary of recommendations
  - For Risk AI: "Your application is being reviewed" + timeline
  - For Demo: "Someone from our team will reach out in 24 hours"
