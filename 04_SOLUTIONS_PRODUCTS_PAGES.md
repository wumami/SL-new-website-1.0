# Solutions & Products Pages: Design Principles & Templates

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

**Solutions = Problems we solve** (outcome-focused)  
**Products = Building blocks** (feature-focused)

### Key Difference:
- **Solution page**: "Launch a crypto exchange in 6 weeks" (speaks to business outcome)
- **Product page**: "Spencer Exchange" (speaks to technical capabilities)

## 🎨 Design Patterns (All Solutions & Products Pages)

### Consistent Elements:

**Hero Section**:
- Always full-width
- Headline + subheadline + CTA
- Screenshot or video on right (desktop)
- Mobile: stack vertically

**Section Padding**:
- Desktop: 64px top/bottom
- Mobile: 32px top/bottom
- Between sections: 96px (desktop), 48px (mobile)

**CTAs**:
- Primary: Spencer blue, white text
- Secondary: Ghost (outline), Spencer blue border
- Always at least 2 CTAs per page (top and bottom)

**Card Styles**:
- Consistent across all pages
- Border: 1px solid #E2E8F0
- Border-radius: 8px
- Padding: 24px
- Hover: lift + shadow increase

**Typography**:
- H1: 48px (desktop), 32px (mobile)
- H2: 36px (desktop), 28px (mobile)
- H3: 24px
- Body: 16px
- All headings: weight 600-700

## 🚫 What NOT to Include

### On Solution Pages:
- ❌ Technical jargon without explanation
- ❌ "Why Spencer Logic" generic marketing
- ❌ Competitor comparison tables (low-value)
- ❌ Generic stock photos of traders

### On Product Pages:
- ❌ Every single feature (prioritize top 6-8)
- ❌ Complex architecture diagrams (unless expandable)
- ❌ Long walls of text (use bullets, cards)
- ❌ "Request pricing" unless transparent pricing available

---

## ✅ Quality Checklist (Every Page)

- [ ] Headline is outcome-focused (not feature-focused)
- [ ] At least 1 case study referenced
- [ ] CTAs are specific (not generic "Learn more")
- [ ] Mobile responsive (test on iPhone, Android)
- [ ] Load time < 2 seconds
- [ ] All links functional
- [ ] Images optimized (WebP, lazy loading)
- [ ] SEO title + meta description present
- [ ] Analytics events tracked (CTA clicks, scroll depth)
